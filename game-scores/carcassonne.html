Points use the current (second) edition rules: a completed two-tile city is worth 4.

Inns & Cathedrals: a road with an inn scores 2 per tile when completed and nothing if unfinished. A city with a cathedral scores 3 per tile and pennant when completed and nothing if unfinished.

Traders & Builders: a pig in your field adds 1 per city (4 instead of 3). At the end, whoever has the most of each trade good (wine, grain, cloth) scores 10 for it; ties all score 10.

The Princess & the Dragon: the fairy's owner scores 1 at the start of their turn and 3 when the meeple next to the fairy scores.

The Tower: buying back a captured meeple costs 3 points, paid to the player who holds it.

Abbey & Mayor: an abbey scores like a monastery. Fields with a barn score 4 per completed city at the end. When a barn joins a field with farmers, those farmers score 3 per city at once; farmers who later join a barn field score 1 per city.

King & Robber: at the end the King scores 1 per completed city in the game and the Robber Baron 1 per completed road.

Hills & Sheep: a completed monastery scores 3 more for each vineyard around it. A shepherd scores the total of the sheep in the flock. Hills only break ties.

Bridges, Castles & Bazaars: a castle scores the points of the first feature completed next to it. Bazaar bids are paid in points: use Other points with a minus sign.

Anything else (more expansions, house rules) can be added with Other points.

Count from photo: take a photo of one feature with its meeples. Claude fills in the calculator (type, tiles, pennants, who has the most meeples) and lists anything it was unsure about. Check the numbers, then press Add to scores.
