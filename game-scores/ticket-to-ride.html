Tickets: a completed ticket adds its value, an unfinished one takes it away.

USA: Longest Continuous Path is worth 10. USA 1910 can also use the Globetrotter, 15 points for the most completed tickets.

Europe: each station you did not place is worth 4 points. The European Express (longest path) is worth 10.

Nordic Countries: 40 trains each. The Globetrotter (most completed tickets) is worth 10; there is no longest path bonus.

Ties for a bonus give the full bonus to everyone tied.

Read tickets from photo: lay out a player's tickets and take a photo. Claude reads the cities and points; you mark each one completed or not before they are added.
