Scoring rules

A game has one round per double, starting with the highest (double-12 for a double-12 set) and ending with the double blank. Double-9 sets play 10 rounds, double-12 sets 13 and double-15 sets 16.

When a player plays their last domino the round ends. Everyone else counts the pips on the dominoes left in their hand and adds them to their score. The player who went out scores 0.

If the round is blocked, everyone counts their pips.

Many tables count the double blank as 50 points when it is left in a hand. Turn this on in the setup if you play that way.

After the last round the player with the lowest total wins.
