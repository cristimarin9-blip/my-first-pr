Scoring rules

The game uses a double-12 set and has 13 rounds, one for each double, starting with double-12 and ending with the double blank.

When a player plays their last domino the round ends. Everyone else counts the pips on the dominoes left in their hand and adds them to their score. The player who went out scores 0. If the round is blocked, everyone counts their pips.

After the last round the player with the lowest total wins.
