Each player has 5 settlements and 4 cities. You start with 2 settlements. Building a city replaces one of your settlements, so use “Upgrade to city”.

Longest Road and Largest Army move by themselves. A player takes the card when they have strictly more than the current holder. If the holder's road is broken and two other players tie for the longest, nobody holds it.

Seafarers: add the island bonus tokens (usually 1 or 2 points each). The points to win depend on the scenario, so set them above.

Cities & Knights: you play to 13. There is no Largest Army. Each metropolis is worth 2 more points on top of its city. “Defender of Catan” cards are 1 point each, and the Merchant is worth 1 point while you control it. Printer and Constitution count as victory point cards.
