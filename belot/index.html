How a hand is scored

Raw points are divided by 10 and rounded. In a suit game 7 and up rounds up (86 → 8, 87 → 9). In all trumps and no trumps 5 and up rounds up.

No declarations or belot count in no trumps. In all trumps every K + Q pair counts as a belot.

Inside: if the team that took the contract scores less than the other team, the other team gets all points from the hand.

Hanging: on a tie the defenders keep their points. The bidders' points carry over to whoever wins the next hand.

Double / redouble: the winning team takes every point of the hand, multiplied by 2 or 4.

Belot is never lost: it always stays with the team that called it. Enter only the declarations that count after comparing sequences at the table.
